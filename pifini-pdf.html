Trellix and Pifini Community

How Trellix rebuilt its customer and partner community from zero with Pifini Community, and turned everyday conversations into training, retention, and measurable savings

Summary

When Trellix formed from the combination of McAfee’s enterprise business and FireEye, it inherited two well-established customer communities. Then it lost both of them in the merger. No platform, no discussion history, no members.

Starting over turned into an opportunity. Trellix used Pifini Community, connected directly to Pifini Learn, to build a single home for customers, partners, and employees.

Trellix also consolidated three separate learning platforms into one and opened training access to thousands of partners who previously couldn’t get it. Within three months of public launch, the new community had grown past 10,000 members.

About Trellix

Trellix is an enterprise cybersecurity company formed from the combination of McAfee’s enterprise business and FireEye. It operates alongside a sister company, Skyhigh Security, and serves customers and partners across every major region.

Trellix Academy is the company’s training and certification hub, and Trellix Community, officially the Trellix Thrive Community, is where customers, partners, and employees connect with peers and subject matter experts.

The Challenge

Trellix formed from the combination of two companies, McAfee’s enterprise business and FireEye. Both had operated active, wellestablished customer communities before the merger. When Trellix formed, that history disappeared along with the platforms themselves, the discussion archives, the member base, all of it. Trellix wasn’t looking to improve a community. It was starting from zero.

The bigger problem sat underneath. Trellix was running four separate learning platforms, uploading the same training multiple times and supporting parallel streams of the same content

for different audiences. Partner access to the content library was capped at 2,000 seats, not by strategy but by the licensing cost of the platform then in use. Thousands of partners who wanted training simply couldn’t get it

Trellix evaluated five to six major learning and community vendors before choosing a path forward. The pattern was consistent: every option was too much community or too much learning management, and nothing was built to do both. One vendor spent three months without ever granting sandbox access, leaving Trellix unable to test whether its requirements were even achievable.

The Solution

One Platform,
Not Two Bolted Together.

Trellix made an architectural decision that shaped everything else: rather than bolting social features onto the learning management system, the company removed the social layer from Trellix Academy entirely and moved it to Trellix Community. Learning content lives in the Academy. Everything social lives in

the Community. The two are linked bi-directionally, share single signon identity, and present as one destination rather than two systems.

A customer facing a configuration problem now searches the Community first. They find peers who’ve hit the same issue, answers from Trellix subject matter experts, and increasingly, direct links to the Academy resource that solves it.

A customer question draws responses from peers and Trellix subject matter experts, and the discussion stays searchable for anyone who hits the same issue later.

One Identity,
Every Audience.

Member segmentation happens at the identity layer instead of through separate community instances. Single sign-on assigns each member a type, employee, partner, or in

customer, and role-based visibility determines what they see. That’s what lets one Community serve Trellix, its partners, and its sister company Skyhigh Security at once, without multiplying administration or splitting the member base.

Community layouts are assembled from a reusable widget library, allowing a single administrator to configure and maintain the experience without development work.

The Outcome

Trellix launched its new community in September 2022. Within three months, it had grown past 10,000 members. The company consolidated four separate learning platforms into one, and opened training access to thousands of partners who previously couldn’t get it.

Trellix is now able to measure the impact of its community on retention, training completion, and support costs. The company is also able to identify and reward its most active contributors, creating a virtuous cycle of engagement that benefits everyone.
